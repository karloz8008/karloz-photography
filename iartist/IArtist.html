Back Up & Move To Another Device

This backs up your WHOLE app — every acting and voiceover audition, every booking, modeling, background jobs, casting directors, and your whole diary book — in one single file. It is all saved inside this browser only. Chrome on your laptop, Safari on your phone and any tablet each keep their own separate copy — they do not talk to each other. To move everything, or to keep it safe, press Back Up Everything here, then open this app on the other device and press Restore From Backup and pick that file.
