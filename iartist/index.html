You are an artist. Your job is to create. Let IArtist remember the rest.

A free app for actors and models. Every audition, every callback, every booking, every dollar, and every casting director, producer and photographer you ever worked with, kept in one place you can open years from now.

It is your accountant and your memory. It lives in your own browser, on your phone, tablet, laptop or desktop, and you can move your data between devices with one backup file. Nobody sees it but you. No account, no subscription, no catch.

Be free. Be yourself, the artist.

Download the free app

On a computer, Mac or Windows

  1. Press the button above. A file called IArtist.html goes to your Downloads folder.
  2. Open your Downloads folder and double-click IArtist.html.
  3. It opens in your browser, ready to use. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave and Safari all work, on older computers too. After this it needs no internet.

On a phone or tablet, iPhone, iPad or Android

  1. Press the button below. The app opens right in your browser.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. From now on open it from your home screen, like any app. Your data stays on your phone.
